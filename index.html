How this works

Six small things a day. Tick off four and the day counts towards your run — you don't have to be perfect for it to be working.

The panel at the top of this page inks itself in as you go. Finish all six and it bursts. If you'd rather draw your own, the Studio tab lets you, and it'll show up here instead.

On the fuel tab It's there so you can see you're eating enough, not so you can eat less. If counting food ever starts to feel stressful, or you catch yourself trying to get the number down, close the tab and tell your mum or dad. That's not you failing at the app — it's the app being the wrong tool that week.

Keeping it safe

Back it up

Everything lives on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clear your browser data and it's gone, so copy a backup somewhere now and then.

Your drawings are stored separately and are not part of the text backup.

Six a day

Today's panels

 

The rule

Four out of six holds the run

Miss a couple and you're still fine. Miss the lot and the run goes back to zero — but so what, you start it again tomorrow. Nobody's ever built anything by having a perfect first month.

Eight weeks

The arcs

Three sessions a week, plus the stretches every day. No equipment, no gym, nothing you can't do on your bedroom floor.

Week 1
Week 1 · Learning to land

Warm up first — every time

Two minutes. March on the spot, roll your shoulders back ten times, swing each leg forwards and back ten times, then roll your ankles. You want to feel slightly warm, not tired.

The session

 

Read this bit Sharp pain means stop — that's different from muscles feeling tired or shaky, which is normal. Sore for a day or two after is normal too. Sore for four days means you did too much, so do less next time, not more.
Every day

The six-minute stretch

Do this any time — after training, before bed, whenever. Hold each one and breathe normally. Never bounce, and never stretch so far it hurts.

Three things about this tab

The bar fills up, it never runs out. There's no number counting down and nothing goes red. Going past the line isn't a problem — that's what the line is for.

Rough numbers are fine. Nobody weighs their toast. Guessing gets you close enough, and close enough is the whole point.

Skipping a day is fine too. This tab is optional. The other four aren't much good if this one is making you miserable.
